How each choice was made

  • Three looksCalm, Soft and Friendly. Friendly won.
  • BudgetsDays' worth, jars, today line. Jars won.
  • ListsSoft glyphs, menu leaders, timeline. Soft glyphs won.
  • Sets and repsScrubber, presets, soft pill. Scrubber won.
  • Rest timerKitchen dial, hourglass, flip clock. Hourglass with quiet buttons won.
  • Budget setup and starting a dayPick your jars, and the round start button.
  • App iconColourways and creative directions. The clay mark won.
